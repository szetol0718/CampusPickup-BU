Welcome to Lee Po Cafe

Lai Bo Café is one of Tuen Mun’s beloved neighborhood cha chaan tengs, serving classic Hong Kong comfort food in a warm, no-frills setting. From silky milk tea and buttery toast to baked pork chop rice and hearty breakfast sets, it’s the kind of place locals visit daily for affordable, fast, and satisfying meals. If you want to experience authentic Hong Kong café culture like a true local, Lai Bo Café is definitely worth a stop.
