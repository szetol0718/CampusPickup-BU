About Confucius

Kong Qiu (551–479 BCE), known in the West as Confucius, was a Chinese philosopher, teacher, and political thinker whose ideas have profoundly influenced East Asian culture, education, and ethics for over two thousand years.

His teachings emphasized moral integrity, respect for tradition, the importance of education, and the cultivation of personal virtue. Central to Confucian thought are values such as righteousness, filial piety, loyalty, and social harmony.

Many of Confucius’s sayings were preserved by his students in a collection known as The Analects, which remains one of the most influential philosophical texts in history.
